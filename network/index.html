I didn't build a $32M car business by bidding at auctions.

Questions

Before you join.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Requesting access is free and there's no obligation to buy. You only pay for the cars you choose to buy.

Who can join?

Licensed motor dealers only. We ask for your dealership name and dealer licence number so the stock stays in the trade.

How are prices set?

Every car has one fixed wholesale price. No offers, no auctions, no "what's your best price". The price you see is the price you pay.

What does "projected retail" mean?

It's the wholesale price plus 25%, shown on every car as a guide to what it could retail for and the margin that leaves you. It's an estimate, not a guarantee, so always run your own numbers.

What if another dealer hits Buy now at the same time?

The first purchase to reach us wins and the car is locked to that dealer instantly. Everyone else sees it marked as sold straight away, so nobody's left guessing.

What happens after I buy?

Our team contacts you to confirm payment and arrange delivery or pickup. You can follow every stage on the car's tracking page and message us from the Chat tab.

Can I ask questions about a car before buying?

Yes. Every listing has an "Ask us about this car" button that opens a chat with our team, or call 1300 08 88 08.
