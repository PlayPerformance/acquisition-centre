Questions

Good to know.

Who can join?

The Acquisition Centre is for licensed motor dealers only. We ask for your dealership name and dealer licence number when you request access.

How are prices set?

Every car has one fixed wholesale price set by Play Performance. There are no offers or auctions; the price you see is the price you pay.

What does "projected retail" mean?

It's the wholesale price plus 25%, shown as a guide to what the car could retail for and the margin that leaves you. It's an estimate, not a guarantee, so always run your own numbers.

What happens if two dealers hit Buy now at once?

The first purchase to reach us wins and the car is locked to that dealer instantly. Everyone else sees it marked as sold.

What happens after I buy?

Our team contacts you to confirm payment and arrange delivery or pickup. You can follow each stage on your car's tracking page and message us any time from the Chat tab.

I forgot my password.

Tap "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in screen and we'll email you a reset link.
